01 — About

A developer who learned to ship under pressure

I graduated from Kent Institute with a Bachelor of Information Technology in 2025, and spent July to October 2026 as a software developer intern at YoungLogix, alongside a Professional Year at Performance Education.

For four months my week was spent in a production Odoo codebase, writing Python and XML for equipment maintenance and inventory modules. It was the first time my code had real users behind it, and it taught me things a university project cannot — reading an unfamiliar codebase, keeping changes reviewable, and asking the question that saves a day of work.

Outside of study, six-plus years in customer-facing roles taught me the unglamorous skills that make teams work: precision, staying calm under load, and explaining a complicated thing to someone simply. I am looking for a junior developer or web development role where I can keep learning from people who build well.
